What Matters

Our generation has been so focused On our individual livelihoods That we are blind to the world Around us, and cannot see or comprehend How our self-centered lives Risk all of us and all life. It’s past time to focus on the legacy We’re inflicting on our progeny. Maybe we can still change ourselves And our ways of living, maybe not. Do we want to be known as The generation that did not care Enough to try, as the generation That fouled our nest because of Ignorance, wrong priorities, Or lack of moral will? If we continue to screw up, The reason won’t matter, Only the results will matter. And matter. And matter.
