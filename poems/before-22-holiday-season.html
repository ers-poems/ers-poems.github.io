Holiday Season - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Holiday Season

The Holiday Season has arrived. I know this because our family gathering Marked the start of it, And not a specific date or Name such as Thanksgiving, Chanukah, or Christmas. My Holiday plans depend upon Are defined by family gatherings, Of children, grandchildren, Siblings, nieces and nephews, Visiting, sharing food together, Sharing feelings and experiences, Rejoicing our separate lives, And expressing our binding love.
