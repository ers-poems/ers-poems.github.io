Weed - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Weed

Thin, scrawny stalk, unpretty seed pods Top the weed growing from a crack in the driveway. How did this random orphan seed manage To sprout and survive in these hostile environs? Never fertilized or fed, given a drink. Often run over or trampled on. It has outlived plants tended with care. Maybe this stubborn weed is Nature’s soldier, Never to surrender, ultimate salute to life.
