Cast Iron Skillet

Nerve ends leak out of my skin Singing every word, Every song, Every little event. Our separation means separating. Divorce promises relief But there is still so much Dividing, tearing apart, to do. You took a few things–rightly so– But one choice scalded my soul– My cast iron skillet, Favorite pan, cared for like a baby’s skin, Used for many things, favorites all. But, your favorite too for one use, So keep it. My wound no longer fresh, pain dulled, Use it well, my wound will heal, And what is one more scar?
