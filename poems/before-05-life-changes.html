Life’s Changes - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Life’s Changes

The brittle air is still and cold As I await the promised snow. All new happenings are welcome To break the doldrums, Quicken my pulse, tingle my life. I now enjoy shared activities, Cooking anything with others, Debating recipes and methods, Remembering and talking about Planning future activities, ventures. Looking together at photos, Albums, scrapbooks, and cards. It is now time to remember, Honor, and cherish our legacy, What we have made.
