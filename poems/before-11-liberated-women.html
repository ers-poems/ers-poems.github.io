Liberated Woman

We all search for identity So we’ll know you from me. Choose a group or inherit one, Or maybe you’ll be one that’s free, Hiding behind no group, no we, Demanding only to be Who you are, not he or she. Think not of WASP, Legion, or Jew, Not of them, but of you. Or are you into Indian or Black? Radical, Reagan, or easy kickback? Then take a look at who you are, You want to be remembered as he or she? Do you want to be liberated Or would you rather be free?
