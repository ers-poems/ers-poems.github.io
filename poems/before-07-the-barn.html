The Barn - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

The Barn

I want to report a missing barn. Yesterday, when the fog lifted, My window’s barn West of V and north of Ocean Had gone missing, Emptying my view of its Centerpiece; a slanting, shuffling barn. Out of farm use, retired to my view, Like an old horse put to pasture, Or a monument to its busy past. How can it just be missing, With only a barn-child shed Left standing to remind artist And my memory’s eye of Our barn picture?
