Memories

For everyone among us, including the poets, I suspect the most reassuring products of our lives Are the memories of triumphs, events, relationships. Indelible pictures, feelings always touching our insides. It is always surprising and rewarding To find our memories are also owned by others. Current events can open a stream of Joint memories from many others, Some out of view for a quarter century, Now recalled fondly with familiarity. Proud is not forgotten, joy lives still, There are no debts, only unconditional offers.
