Holiday Memories, Longings

My holidays are suffused with memories of: Trying to harvest mistletoe from A tall tree with a shotgun, Gathering running cedar from the Ground to make a homespun wreath, Struggling past midnight with “Some assembly required” toys, Searching the woods for a right height Tree with proper shape, no bare spots, Getting all the tree lights to work. Thinking of things I’ve missed: Going on a sleigh ride, Joining a group of carolers, Offering food and drink to Strangers walking the streets, Getting to visit my grandparents.
