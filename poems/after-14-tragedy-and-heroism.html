Tragedy and Heroism - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Tragedy and Heroism

To become a hero requires a dangerous opponent To trigger the will, excite the challenge, Of joying in the mortal confrontation, Embracing the newborn feelings of achievement, Struggle and the chance to compete. To become a hero is not about winning or losing. It is about the strength of the struggle, The nature of the will to survive, And the depth of determination. What a life-awakening event, Ultimate measure of glory.
