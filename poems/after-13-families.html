Families - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Families

I belong to three separate families; The one I was born into, blood-related, The ones I helped start with wives and children, And the one I have chosen to belong to. The last group includes those I have bonded with, Intimate friends, fellow classmates, co-workers, All sharing interests, concerns, affection, respect. Any crisis broadcasts an alert to families, Which is not only a warning, but a cry for help. When the unexpected strikes us. Families rally, circle the wagons, For family members are the ones to send for when Members of my family are not just the best, But the only line of defense and support I need. Thanks to my families, I am not alone.
