Virtual Letter - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Virtual Letter

For the first time in the two months since I learned of my cancer, My mood has changed, my outlook unsure. I feel the need to talk with a friend, to be comforted, reassured. Has the suspected trouble with my lungs or the addition of a third chemical to my treatments unnerved me a little? Or am I just a little lonely? I want to call a friend, but I do not want to give anyone the impression that my positive outlook or determination is weakening. At this time, even asking to talk to a friend, Seems to me to indicate some kind of weakness or that I need help just to maintain my optimism. As much as I would enjoy calling a friend, I do not want to cross the line into an admission of doubt, worry, or despair. Rationally, I know that I am in serious jeopardy medically. But I also know that cancer treatments have improved significantly in recent years. Many cancer patients now go into remission or live years longer than expected. My outcome is in doubt. But what I realize after writing this virtual letter is that I must not let doubt invade my mind or touch my soul. For everyone’s sake, and especially for mine, I must continue to use, appreciate, and enjoy all the moments of all the days I have.
