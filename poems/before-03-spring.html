Spring

There are quasi seasons where I live And in April I miss the explosion of spring. Seeing the blanket of D.C.’s cherry tree blossoms, The first yellow eruption of Forsythias, Fallen Robin’s egg returned to the nest, Newly planted garden’s first green sprouts, Newly born farm animals bleating their welcome, Unique aroma of freshly turned, fecund soil. Majestic waterfalls swollen from melted snow, Life-affirming showers of rain, gentle breezes, The crack of baseball bats–reminding Optimism, selective memories, and new hopes.
