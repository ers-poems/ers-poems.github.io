Recovery - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Recovery

This is a serious situation I’m in, Featuring cancer, chemo, and blood clots. It is a constant reminder of mortality, But also stimulates me to keep searching for: The surprise smile of a friend or relative, The thankful birds enjoying my bird feeder, The wonderful deeds, hopes for grandchildren, The admirable parents my children have become, Our family’s solidarity, trust, respect, love. I will cherish these things and all the other Experiences I have for as long as I have.
