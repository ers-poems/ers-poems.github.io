The Gift of Giving

I remember when Holiday gifts were fewer, chosen with anxiety, treasured. Now the corporate mega-stores focus exclusively, blindly, on profits, diluting and obscuring our Holiday season. We can reclaim it, define our celebration, not limited to buying so many things. Instead, offer treasures made with your own hands, or give your time. Make something, almost anything–a carved wooden toy, a crocheted cover, A knitted garment, a painted picture, a photo album or scrapbook, a poem, or a new story. Or give a coupon good for: A sleepover or swim party, three sewing repairs, preparing two gourmet meals, or hosting a family gathering. Risk giving your talent and time to take back our Holidays.
