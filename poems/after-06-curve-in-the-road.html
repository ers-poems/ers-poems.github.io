Curve in the Road

What a comfortable, protective security blanket! Almost five months of progressive chemo, Good, measurable results, mother of hope, Optimism for satisfactory results, remission. What a surprise setback: failed chemo! Symptoms are worse, future alarming. Everything is in doubt, plans on hold. It’s not the end, but a new beginning. A new chemo starts next week. If one doesn’t work, try another. Now there is no security blanket, Just unflinching determination To continue the struggle, never give in.
