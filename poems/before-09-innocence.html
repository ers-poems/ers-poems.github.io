Innocence - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Innocence

Every new baby comes to us perfect, Capable of true emotions only, Crying for food, or in pain, Or looking totally vulnerable And seeing only with God-like eyes everything, Surprised to be disappointed, To be ignored, for a second. We start with perfection, What a burden! We can only Descend– We try to do as little harm as possible, Yet, every word, every gesture, Every touch means so much more Than we wanted or intended. We hold innocence in our lap. For such a precious time We are lost in it, and try to Lose it slowly.
