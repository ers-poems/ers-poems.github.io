My Friend, My Doctor

I have a few reliable friends, bonded to each other, always within reach, for whatever need or deed, the ones to send for, when. Like many of us, I used to think Doctors weren't real people, but robots–Unwelcome, but sometimes necessary inconveniences to be endured. A patient and Doctor, friends? Oxymoron indeed! Then I met you, then knew you–Man, Father, Brother, Priest–And Doctor of Medicine too. It's way too much; too few could imagine, much less do what it takes to be a friend. It has happened; thus it can be. What a model for Doctor/patient, what an expression of love was the hug you offered me!
