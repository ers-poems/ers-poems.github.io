Mother

What do you think of first, When remembering your life? The ennobling experiences of pride? The wretched times, harking still loud? My life bleeds slowly, steadily And knows its source. The flow is not you but me And my time, my course. My beginning and my start Are only a part– Yet there is something there That is your breath, my air. So close to you, So separately, So much of you, So much of me.
