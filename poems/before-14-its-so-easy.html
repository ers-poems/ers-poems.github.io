It’s So Easy - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

It’s So Easy

Age alone dictates our last time around, Too late to hope for endless chances to get it right. Our ways are set, habits on autopilot, Views clear with no argument or apology. We just are; took a long time to get here, We don’t want to be anywhere else. Too much effort and no incentive. Each day we celebrate who and what we are– Saying our raw feelings and thoughts Without any worry or concern for Acceptance or approval. It’s always okay to be tired or depressed or anxious, To have too much wine or too much of anything, Or not enough. There’s no longer anyone to impress, And no need to impress ourselves. We don’t have the time or energy or desire To be anyone else. We are happy with who we are, Or at least have settled for it contentedly. It’s so easy– To call anytime To touch anywhere, To speak anything, To give what I want, To take what you offer, To hold tightly, To let loose, To miss you away, To come back today, To be so comfortable, To not miss love’s angst. It’s so easy– This time around.
