Breath - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Breath

I awake late at night, hyperventilating, Struggling for air with desperate breathing. Quickly it stops; physically it's okay. Emotionally, I’ve been painfully reminded That I am vulnerable to lung cancer. This attention-getting event struck me As a result of the recent lung failure And death of a family member. He had ALS, not lung cancer. Rationally, no connection, similar fear. My optimism again tested by close reality, I’ve survived other tests, now Another one to pass, outlive, forget.
