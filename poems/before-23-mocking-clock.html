Mocking Clock

Beeping sound from my guest room Woke me suddenly; he had Left his alarm clock on Before he left and it chattered To me in a high, repeating pitch. Since that time, I have been awakened Once or twice every night By a clear, perfect rendition of the clock’s Alarm notes sung by my resident mockingbird. The mockingbird faithfully includes The alarm tune with all his other songs, Waking me up so I can hear And relish his full musical repertoire.
