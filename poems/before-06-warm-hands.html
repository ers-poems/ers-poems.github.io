Warm Hands - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Warm Hands

I have warm hands. Another told me so last night. I hold onto this As a raft in turbulent water, Desperate I am for any approval. I need any good I can come by. No good came from my youth, Just an empty hunger For strokes, signs of worth. I am still looking for these. But maybe something will change. My hands are still warm.
