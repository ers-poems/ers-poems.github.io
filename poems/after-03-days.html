Days - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Days

Now each day is not only measured, But consumed, relished, treasured. We think about things we still want to do, Favorites to enjoy once more, Or going somewhere or doing something We’ve never done before, but Thought vaguely about doing. Not vaguely anymore—either or—decide. I would like another dinner at Downey’s in Santa Barbara and One at Chez Panisse in Oakland. I would like another visit to Pike Place Market in Seattle, and to Chino’s vegetable farm near San Diego, I would like to prepare one more meal With fresh Morels and White King Salmon. There could be days for the above But not enough to be at the Masters Golf Tournament at Azalea And dogwood time in Augusta.
