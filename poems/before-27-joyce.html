Joyce - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Joyce

Some among us know how thin Is the edge of life. We have sweated and trembled Waiting for those lab results, Shuddered at the Doctor’s approach, Been laid open to naked mortality. The humbling experience, No appeal, no recourse. None of us was promised fair, Why did we expect it? But some of us are blessed, Beyond why. We survive the “C” word, Enrich our honesty, endear our moments. I will forever remember The lilt in your voice, The sparkle in your eyes I couldn’t see, That overwhelmed, touched, uplifted me. Who was comforting whom? We will be, be again, and still be, Our time will come, but it is not yet. (Written for Jim’s dear friend, Joyce Lacey)
