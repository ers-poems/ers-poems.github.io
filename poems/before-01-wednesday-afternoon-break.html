Wednesday Afternoon Break - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Wednesday Afternoon Break

I spent the afternoon under the sun, Lying on my back in the grass, Hearing the squeaky leaves Of Eucalyptus trees Dancing in the breeze. I could hear the air Step from tree to tree Until it reached me. I wanted to reach up And pull the foamy clouds Down over me. It was so quiet there I could hear my own sounds. I did not sound as soft As the rustling grass, Nor as shrill as the Ruffled bird above. I think I sounded warm As this workday ends.
