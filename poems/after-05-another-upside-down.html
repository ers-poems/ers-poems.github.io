Another Upside Down

Sitting in a chair, tubes in my chest, immobile, For five hours. Sounds boring, huh? For me now Nothing is any longer boring. Each moment, minute, Hour, is cherished, jealously processed. Everyday things grab my attention, My soul spills out with feelings, My mind only turns off with sleep. Memories and thoughts tumble over each other With no separation of time. I can just sit and stare out the window, Absorb the view, the plants, the birds, For as long as I want and need to.
