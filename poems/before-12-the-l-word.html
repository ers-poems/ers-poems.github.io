The “L” Word - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

The “L” Word

Maybe six decades ago, when I was cringing At nearly every word and certainly every slap, Maybe the “love” word would have righted My life, secured me from fear and warned my soul. Maybe. Six decades later the “love” word has become as Routine as saying “Hello.” It is now proclaimed to Everyone in line of sight or within hearing By anyone. Talk about inflation! The word is so cheap that It means less than a drop of water in a rainstorm. We need a word which means something, which Reflects our endearment, one to the other, which approaches What we mean to each other. A word which the world Cannot vitiate by overuse. A special word. An “L” word. Like, not love. I am in like. It is respect, integrity, Honesty, admiration, and fun. What more could there be? What more could you want? L.
