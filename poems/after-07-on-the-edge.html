On The Edge - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

On The Edge

My oncologist is pleased With the chemo results. Hope springs to a new life. My hand on the doorknob, Anxious to see the other side. I don’t expect to see certainty, I hope to see the possibilities: Ones that might include remission, Ability to make some trips, Resume doing favorite things, And finding new favorites.
