Rounded Words - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Rounded Words

Through all our rounded words Coated smoothly by both of us, To protect and insulate, We both knew and sensed Something too rare for us. Had tender feelings slipped our lips, A direct grasping of eyes broken The bonds of circumstance– But too late, the cost too great– We’ll always speak in rounded words.
