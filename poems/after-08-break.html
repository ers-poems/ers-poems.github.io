Break

I’m traveling a long, uphill road with no end in sight. I expected this, but I would really like a short detour Where for a day or two I have no pain and some energy. But this is not to be for now; I must plod on, no break. To keep my spirits up, I must plan for, enjoy: Small things, a good bridge game, a good book, News from my family and friends, anticipation of visits. The road is long, but has a destination. Travel on.
