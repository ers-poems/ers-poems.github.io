Mom - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Mom

Thank you for– Sacrificing your wants and needs–your ration stamps, the clothes you did without. For working past pain and exhaustion, never stopping until you were satisfied. Letting me be responsible, trusting me–and for forgiving my youthful mistakes. For always demanding I do my best and never settling for anything less. For teaching me to read before I went to school and always encouraging me to keep my aim high. For making a home in appalling conditions, providing care, protection, and family traditions. May you be blessed in the innumerable ways you have blessed us. And may you revel in the esteem That is your due, is offered to you, on this Mother's Day.
