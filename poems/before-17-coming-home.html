Coming Home - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Coming Home

Coming home together marks the Holiday Season. The first sight of each of you, smiles erupting, Eyes glowing, arms open, inviting embrace– Let the celebration commence! And, may you choose to, please– Save an ornament for me for the tree, Set a place at the table for me, Reserve a course on the menu for my offering, Leave one candle in the Menorah for me to light, And keep a little space under the tree For my clumsily wrapped gifts, Chosen with delightful agony, a little trepidation. Oh! Let us rejoice! We will talk and touch And be touched. We will dine and share, All will be there! Thank you for the joy and love flowing All around me, caressing my soul– Happy Holidays!
