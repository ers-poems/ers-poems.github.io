No Shortcuts - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

No Shortcuts

After almost a year, only the next chemo Event is dependable; cannot count on anything else. So, I will plan to use non-chemo weeks to Have dinner parties, play bridge at night, Travel with a hired companion—if necessary— Maybe even go on a cruise, or visit For me, some new exciting place. The most formidable obstacle I have Is fatigue and shortness of breath. This just needs imagination and Observing intelligent limits, asking for help, Resting, resting, resting and relishing the possible.
