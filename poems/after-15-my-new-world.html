My New World

There may be no more dramatic event than a Perilous threat to a person’s health To rekindle memories, longing and support From family and friends, even long-ago friends. Things taken for granted, assumed always there, Are now in doubt, may soon be gone, Words left unspoken now cry out to be heard, Old negative thoughts, opinions, feelings, Whether real or imagined, dissolve, Become irrelevant in an egoless world, Where there are all new visions and values. Some part of our nature—self-defense?— Needs the fear of loss to shock into awareness, The value of appreciation and love For the newly needful patient.
