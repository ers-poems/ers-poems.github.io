Thanksgiving

As an adult, my favorite holiday Has always been Thanksgiving. Maybe it‘s because there are no presents, Or cards or religious events involved. But really I believe it is because Thanksgiving features the best opportunity To express our love of family by The care we take to plan and enjoy Our meal courses and traditions. I have no childhood memories Of a festive Thanksgiving. We only ate what we could grow Or barely afford, no luxury of tradition. Maybe that’s part of my overwhelming Desire to serve the best I can.
