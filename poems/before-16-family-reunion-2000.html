Family Reunion, 2000 - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Family Reunion, 2000

It is ennobling, this proud lump of emotion Squeezing from the stomach to the throat, Touching our souls, on the way to togetherness. We flock as a family, assistant midwives To the new millennium born to us. Like migrating birds gone to their origin We gather at the symbolic beginning Of life’s journey, celebrating who we are, Have been, and will be. We are allowed this celebration, even owe it To ourselves, our children and our children’s Children–why else are we here? In the coming years, maybe technology will Voice-phone us together, But it will never replace A cheerful grandchild with lucid eyes That see only the truth of purity. The togetherness we feel, memories Evoked when siblings hug in greeting. The triumph we feel when comforting A nephew or niece in a little hurt now. As we return home, May memories of this meeting Help define our family more, Help us realize all that family can be.
