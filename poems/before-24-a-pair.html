A Pair

Now no surprise from moments rare For we welcome and treasure Each one still and we will Take the dare–be a pair As moments sum and as they wear. Moments are threaded unto ties As they are spoken and shared, Tried on for fit or join, As we see, know when we Fit together in shape and form.
