Father to Sons - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Father to Sons

Ah! I was twenty- seven once, And I could do anything In barely a moment, And for as long as any could stand. I was a minor god to me And the father of you three. I loved you with strength And tenderness, I hope. It is not right for a father To correct his life On the shoulders of his sons, And this, I hope, I haven’t done. Yet, pardon my vision Or a parent’s view For all the best I am I see in you.
