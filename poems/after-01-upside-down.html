Upside Down

The CT scan was finished, The ER Doctor on duty told me She had good news—no blood clot— But also bad news–lung cancer. It took a day on so for my world To turn upside down, inverting my life. Taking care of chores such as renewing My driver’s license, doing my taxes, Cleaning things up, or paying the bills On time vanished from my radar screen. It became trivial to spend money To fix inconvenient problems, mine Or those of my children. I still care about Iraq, global warming, And universal healthcare for Americans, But they are no longer even on My list of personal priorities. Taking care of myself jumped to priority one. Visiting family and friends is no longer Just pleasant, taken for granted, but vital. Taking the most from each day is what I have, Not to be wasted, but to be totally enjoyed.
