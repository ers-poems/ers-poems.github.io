Heroines and Heroes - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Heroines and Heroes

Do not boast of those who garner accolades, Whose deeds are known and publicized, Rewarded by being recognized, Who are shielded by an aura of fame, Their egos reinforced by general acclaim. Save some regard for those Whose heroic deeds measure no less But remain anonymous, unknown success– Who quietly perform and sacrifice, Who trust and do no artifice. These are the ones who take on the load On their own without being told, Who look for no escape, will not fold– Whose path leads only to one goal To finish the job, to honor the soul. Being done, and well, does not long last, For on the way to the exhausting finish Their efforts did others fairly diminish– The others scramble for pieces of fame While the silent heroes go as they came.
