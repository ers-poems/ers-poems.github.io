Gasping - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Gasping

We each have three physical necessities: Food, water, and oxygen. We can survive with no food for quite a while, Weeks—even, and without water For days, maybe. Both cases Affording ample time for thought, Actions to remedy our situation. Not so with oxygen; we have only A few collapsing minutes to find relief. This is panic time, requiring emotional control, And immediate, rational response. Time is critical in one sense, Irrelevant in another. The only relief is to breathe again. What a relief!
