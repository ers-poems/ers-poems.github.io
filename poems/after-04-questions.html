Questions - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Questions

My number one conundrum is to find out: What is going on inside my own body? What are the numbers for red and white blood cells? How much has the cancer been reduced? What is the condition of my lungs? Is the new chemical, Avastin, working? When will I be ready for the next scan? When will I be able to travel again? Will I be able to attend my daughter’s wedding? Will I be able to witness my granddaughter’s Bat Mitzvah? I live every day with these unknowns. I am not personally worried about any of them. But I am ultimately concerned about all of them. My outlook and attitude are that I look Forward to the arrival of pleasant surprises.
