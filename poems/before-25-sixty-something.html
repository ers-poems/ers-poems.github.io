Sixty Something - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Sixty Something

Like a river, our journeys to here Have foamed over rocks, Swirled too long in eddies, Swallowed by rapids sometimes, Only to be thrown free. Now, we search for calm waters, Where we can delight in reflected lights, And ease our tired feet In cool, clear water. Now we will wade the last bend Together, hands holding hands, Barefoot with pants rolled up, Ready to be wherever The river goes.
