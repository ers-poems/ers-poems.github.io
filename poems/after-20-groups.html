Groups

I know a few restrictive, limited groups Created by chance, with exclusive criteria. These groups include those together in the Trenches of battle, parents who have lost children, And cancer patients with unknown prognosis. Members of these random groups Can relate to and empathize with Only members of the same group. For words alone are not enough: One must have lived through like events To lay the foundation for understanding. It helps for each group’s members To discover one another, kindred souls. I rejoice in finding members of my group.
