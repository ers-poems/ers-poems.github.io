September 11th - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

September 11th

Emotional rape has scarred us forever. Never again will we feel as before when We board a commercial airline plane, We see turban-wrapped Arabs unexpectedly, We enter an elevator in a skyscraper, Feel as anonymous, safe, in a large public group, Witness a passing funeral procession, Glimpse smoke pouring from a building, Hear the approaching wail of emergency sirens, Decide what can be packed for an airline trip, Give blood to the Red Cross, Write to a relative in uniform, Read of the acts of religious fanatics, Ever feel insulated from distant world tragedies.
