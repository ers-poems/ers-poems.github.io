Apprehension

The first chemo phase is nearing an end, The next scan for cancer looms soon. The protection provided by a remote time Is now turning into one growing apprehension As news of the next outcome closes in. I believe it’s natural, indeed inevitable, To be concerned, worried about the unknown, But since it’s beyond my control I know I need to continue the resolve To welcome each day, enjoy the moment. The challenge is to maintain my direction As the curves, hills, and bumps happen. I will steer on and on. In the same direction.
