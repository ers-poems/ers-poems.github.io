Gifts

I have learned each new day is not for granted, It is to delight in things previously unaware. Every day now presents surprise gifts: Watching the parade of proud Goldfinches, Receiving an email from a long-lost friend, Anticipating another visit from my children, Watching a grandchild invent a new activity, Welcoming the ocean breeze as the sun sets, Being excited by fields of shocking colors, Finding a dependable supply of Meyer lemons, Treasuring every moment of family gatherings, Sharing memories of unspoken experiences, Awakening each day anxious for its new gifts. May you find precious gifts this Holiday Season.
