Life - a poem by Ernest R. Shifflett

Life

I spend a lot of time now Looking for affirmation of life. This is easy; life is ubiquitous, Looking for me, even my guests’ birds. House Finches, Song Sparrows, Black-headed Grosbeaks, Purple Finches, California Towhees, Northern Mockingbirds, Western Scrub Jays, California thrashers, Wrentits, Wilson’s Warblers, Lesser Goldfinches, Morning Doves, And American Goldfinches. I wait for and look for the next New life from nature’s supply.
