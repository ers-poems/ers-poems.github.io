Dedication - Upside Down — Poetic Diary of Ernest R. Shifflett

In Remembrance of

Ernest Rodney Shifflett

1935 - 2008

This is a collection of poems he wrote that he wanted put into a book to be left for his children, grandchildren, brothers and sisters, and friends. I started working on this book for him back in January of 2008, and he told me how he wanted it done. Alina Chekalina, his granddaughter, designed and painted the cover. Thank you, Alina, for doing an outstanding job on this. I know he was very proud of you. Unfortunately, we didn’t finish it before he left us, but I am honored to be able to finish it as a tribute to him and the wonderful person he was. Just by reading his poetry, you will see that he has left a big part of himself with us.

June Shifflett Mitchell
